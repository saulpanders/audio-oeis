OEIS Audio Sequencer

Turn integer sequences from the On-Line Encyclopedia of Integer Sequences (OEIS) into layered, exportable music.

Each track fetches a sequence by its OEIS ID and maps the terms to pitched notes. Three mapping modes are available:

  • MIDI — values map directly to MIDI note numbers (mod 128).
  • Diatonic — values step through a chosen scale and root, staying in key.
  • Microtonal — successive term ratios drive continuous frequency shifts.

Stack as many tracks as you like, set BPM, mute or solo individual tracks, and export the result as WAV.

Some sequences to try: A000045 (Fibonacci), A000040 (primes), A000217 (triangular numbers), A001006 (Motzkin numbers).
